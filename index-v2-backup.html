What your site is costing you — Nevada Residential Services

I'm Jarrett, a Las Vegas web designer. I went through your site the way a homeowner with a dead AC would: search, open a result, try to get help. Your phone number, 24-hour message and request form are easy to find. The leaks are service pages that jump while loading, a slow homepage, an out-of-date site map and mismatched numbers.

Checked October 15, 2026

What I found

01

Your service pages jump around while they load.

On the AC Repair, Furnace Repair and Emergency Plumbing pages, the text slides down after a picture finally arrives. In a simulated slow-connection load test, the layout shift scored 0.86 on AC Repair (the same in both runs), 0.86 on Furnace Repair and 0.85 on Emergency Plumbing (one run each). Google treats anything above 0.25 as poor. The test points to an in-article picture with no set size. Speed scores on those pages were 23 and 22 (AC Repair, two runs), 27 and 37 out of 100.

02

The homepage is slow to settle.

In a simulated slow-connection load test, the main content showed at 4.9 seconds (5.0 on a second run) and the page was blocked from responding for 1,240 milliseconds (810 on the second run). It loads 3.7 MB across 126 requests. The main picture is a background image the browser finds late, which accounts for 3.4 of those 4.9 seconds. Advertising, analytics and chat scripts account for about 1,260 milliseconds of blocked time. Speed score 54 and 58 out of 100. One run on each of the other pages; field speed is unmeasured.

03

Your site map is out of date and leaves out plumbing.

The site map was made with Screaming Frog 11.2, and 447 of its 448 addresses are dated 2021 (one 2020). It doesn't list any of the nine plumbing service pages or the plumbing overview, and none of the six neighborhood AC repair pages your homepage links to. 19 of its 448 addresses don't load directly: 13 are capital-letter duplicates that redirect, one leads to the 404 page, and two are malformed and returned 403.

04

The page shows four different review counts.

The homepage says “Over 1000 5-Star Reviews”, “5.0 797 reviews” beside “5.0 493 reviews”, “5-star Reviews from 600+ customers!” and “745 Verified Ratings” (Yelp 405, Google 336, HomeAdvisor 4). I didn't open the listings, so I can't say which is current.

05

The business details in the page code have errors.

The phone number is the text “[+702.504.4625]” with square brackets, the hours are stored as a text string, one field name is lowercase so it won't be read, and the founding date is “2006.05.01” while your Summerlin page says “since 2015”. A second block, the audio file, can't be parsed and its link is “https://nrs.vegasAir-conditioning-service.mp3” with a missing slash. Three phone numbers are on every page (702.935.0777, 702.504.4625, and a hidden 702.941.7888); the markup uses 702.504.4625.

06

Some buttons and fields are hard to use.

My accessibility check found white text on your red (#fe0000) buttons at a 4.02 contrast ratio where 4.5 is expected, on six buttons including the footer call button. Two links have no name, the service dropdown in the schedule form has no label, the zoom setting stops visitors pinching to zoom, and 110 of the 112 pages I opened have no main-content marker.

07

A few page-level slips.

None of the 112 pages has a canonical tag, and copies of the homepage load at /?service=las-vegas, /?service=henderson and /?service=heating-furnace-repair. Four pages have no description and four have two main headings. robots.txt blocks five Henderson addresses; three of them are live, open to Google and in your site map. It also blocks a misspelled address, /plumbing/drain-cleanign, which is a leftover page titled “Plumbing serives”.

What's working

Your phone number is at the top of every page as a tap-to-call link under “24 hours emergency service”, with a short request form (name, email, phone, zip, service, problem) protected by reCAPTCHA, a chat widget, financing, a membership club and special offers. You have a page for nearly every service (cooling, heating, nine plumbing, air quality), six neighborhood pages, three ZIP-code pages, and recent job entries dated up to October 16, 2026. 111 of 112 pages I opened loaded, nearly all have their own title and description, and the site runs over a secure connection. Your license number is on the homepage.

Unmeasured

Visitor numbers, rankings, how the site looks and loads on a phone (untested), whether the request form and chat reach someone, who answers each phone number, how many reviews you have right now, whether the redirect from http to https works, and speed on pages other than the homepage and three service pages. I didn't submit any forms or look at private data.

What I checked on your site

  • Found
    Not blocking AI crawlers

    Your robots.txt doesn't block any crawler I tested. I tested 11 well-known AI and search crawler names (GPTBot, ChatGPT-User, OAI-SearchBot, ClaudeBot, Claude-SearchBot, PerplexityBot, Google-Extended, Applebot-Extended, CCBot, Bytespider, Amazonbot) and none is blocked.

  • Found
    Page text is readable without scripts

    The homepage HTML contains about 2,000 words of text and one clear heading (“AC Repair & Installation in Las Vegas | 24/7 Emergency HVAC service”), so a crawler that doesn't run JavaScript can still read it.

  • Found
    Your Google listing and profiles are linked

    The page links to a Google Maps search for your address, and the markup lists Facebook, Instagram and Yelp; the page also links YouTube and HomeAdvisor.

  • Gap
    Business markup has errors

    Your homepage has local-business markup with your address, map position and areas served, but the phone is written as “[+702.504.4625]”, the hours as a text string and one field under a name search tools don't read. A second block describing your audio file can't be parsed.

  • Gap
    Plumbing and neighborhood pages aren't in the site map

    The site map is dated 2021 and doesn't list any of your nine plumbing pages or six neighborhood AC repair pages, which your homepage links to.

  • Gap
    Three phone numbers

    The header shows 702.935.0777 and 702.504.4625, and a third number, 702.941.7888, sits in a hidden link on every page. Your address (4620 Arville St Suite B, Las Vegas, NV 89103) is the same on the pages I checked.

  • Gap
    No llms.txt file

    nrs.vegas/llms.txt returns a “not found” page.

These are plain checks anyone can repeat on October 16, 2026: what the site publishes, not a score.

What I couldn't measure

  • Rankings in Google or Google Maps.
  • What ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity or Google's AI answers say when asked directly. I didn't ask those assistants myself, so the searches above are a stand-in.
  • How many people search for these terms or how many visit your site.
  • Your Google listing itself: its reviews, photos, hours and phone number as Google shows them.
  • How your site looks and loads on a phone. I did not test a phone, so mobile is untested.

What I'd fix first

Set a size on the pictures inside your service pages so they stop jumping, and load the homepage's main picture first. Rebuild the site map so it lists the plumbing and neighborhood pages and drops the redirecting and broken addresses. Pick one set of review numbers, fix the phone and hours fields in the page code, and give the red buttons enough contrast. I can't promise where it will place you.
